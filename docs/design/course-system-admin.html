Courses

Seven admin screens for authoring courses and their material, and for seeing who is doing them. Drawn in the admin app's own system — Geist, white surfaces, #171717 primary, #E5E5E5 borders, 10px radius — with Abode teal as the only accent. Two sidebar items: Courses and Learners. Certification is not a third thing; it is a course whose completion grants a credential. Every field drawn here is listed as a data contract at the end.

1. Courses

Every course in one table. First sale path and Certification are role tags, not separate features — a course earns them from its own settings. Sold-out concepts do not get their own nav item.

What this file is not. It does not cover the associate-facing side — taking a course, reading a module, sitting the quiz. Those are separate screens in abode-fe-v2 under Sell, and the dashboard checkpoint that links to them. It also does not decide whether certification should ever be required to sell; that switch is deliberately absent until someone chooses.

Chrome. Drawn in abode-fe-admin's own theme — white surfaces, #171717 primary and text, #E5E5E5 borders, #FAFAFA sidebar, 10px radius, Geist — with Abode teal #0593A5 as the single accent. Sidebar groups mirror components/layout/Sidebar.tsx plus one addition, Academy. Estate figures (₦19,000, ₦205,200, 36 payments) are Abode's real published ones; associates, scores and dates are invented.
